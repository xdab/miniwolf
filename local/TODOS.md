## Tone-detector window

The Goertzel tone detectors integrate over ~3.2 bit periods where matched
integration is ~1 bit: the long window buys SNR but adds ISI, and the
carrier-frame phase-error finding says losses come from slow phase wander
—which longer, cleaner integration averages out. A ~1-bit window with good
clock recovery is the candidate; it is a demod-interface change and must
be scored paired-seed against the recorded baseline.

## Race winner order and selection

The default race delivers frames first-wins in demod-bit order, so
goertzel-optim (bit 0) shadows the others regardless of merit.
Measured: quad-first (`--demods quad,optim`) is PER-neutral at the knee
and identical elsewhere — quadrature completes more frames including
marginal ones that pre-empt optim's clean decodes, and costs more CPU —
so optim-first stands. Remaining ideas, each a paired-seed A/B against
the recorded race baseline: (b) select the winning frame per frame by
demod confidence instead of completion order (needs the soft-bit path);
(c) deliver ALL demods' frames and let dedup collapse them, so order
stops mattering. The race currently beats every solo arm, so any change
must be PER-neutral-or-better, not just cleaner.

## Matched-filter correlator demodulator

The current demods estimate instantaneous frequency (quad) or single-bin
energy (Goertzel) per symbol; neither correlates against the 1200/2200 Hz
symbol templates, which is the standard way to buy low-SNR margin in AFSK
— the analogue of rxx_lora's dechirp-then-FFT: exploit the deterministic
symbol shape instead of just its energy. This is also the receiving end
of the measured failure mode (tools/carrier_frame.py): whole phase
rotations never occur; what kills the receiver is slow carrier-frame
phase wander — noise FM over the frame — exactly what a
correlating/averaging detector tracks through. Intended shape: a
`demod_corr.c`-style variant producing soft symbol confidence alongside
the hard bit, slotted behind the existing demod interface so the
regression suite (tools/regression.py) can score it against the
incumbents on equal footing. The far end: IF/baseband-domain detection
(matched filter or MLSE over the FM waveform) — larger gain, but it
needs a known deviation, a carrier/tone-phase reference and CFO
tracking; only worth building if the correlator direction is
exhausted.

## Frequency-error tolerance and the preamble CFO tracker

Three distinct impairments, previously conflated in the tooling and here:

- **FM RF carrier offset δ** — discriminator output is audio + DC pedestal
  of δ/deviation; the tone pair never shifts. Measured FM-true (pedestal
  added post-discriminator), the RX chain decodes uncorrected across the
  whole realistic carrier-error range: the pedestal is structurally
  rejected (quad mixes DC up to 1700 Hz, above its I/Q LPF cutoff;
  Goertzel's DC leakage cancels in the mark−space difference). No DC
  blocker justified on this evidence.
- **AFSK tone-pair offset h** (the data carriers themselves off nominal) —
  reachable via SSB-style translation, not via FM RF offset. The tools'
  `cfo_offset()` arm models this with real-multiply mirror-image twins,
  which no real path produces: the images manufactured a fragility band
  that vanishes once they are removed (analytic translation). Raw tolerance
  without images covers a few hundred Hz of common shift; beyond that, the
  **flag-symbol preamble tracker** restores decode: correlate the preamble
  span against the deterministic on-air flag template (0x7E NRZI is
  M×7 S×1 repeating — 7 marks then 1 space per 8 bits — synthesizable
  RX-side without any mark/space or alignment assumption), swept over
  candidate offsets; validated to measure h exactly at coarse grid
  resolution, and paired trials show a shift the raw receiver cannot
  decode is decoded after the −h correction. Correction knob: recenter the
  demod detectors (Goertzel `coeff` recompute; quad LO phase increment) or
  an input-side rotation. Trigger: flag detection in the deframer, with a
  small lag search for the preamble start.
- **Proportional clock error** (TX/RX soundcard or TNC crystal) — scales
  tones AND baud together by (1+ε), not a common shift; the RX decodes raw
  at percent-level ε, and `--baud` already models the TX half.

Actions: re-derive the `cfo_offset()` arm (analytic translation, no image
twins; or name a path that produces them before keeping them); encode the
pedestal immunity and the tracker as regression cases so neither silently
regresses. The rxx_lora pattern (measure CFO at sync, recenter the LO) is
the design for the planned RTL-SDR backend, where zero-IF I/Q makes CFO
real and in-band; the flag-symbol tracker is its AFSK instantiation.

Related codebase facts, recorded because they reshape several entries: the
Goertzel detectors are quantized to integer FFT bins in `grz_init`
(`src/goertzel.c`) — at 48 kHz the mark/space detectors sit at
1142.9/2285.7 Hz, not 1200/2200, and an unquantized-detector variant
paired-seed A/B'd WORSE under the image-twin tone-shift arm, so the
off-nominal placement is load-bearing until that arm is re-derived
(re-derive quantization and arm together). The "tone-detector window"
entry's "~3.2 bit periods" premise is stale: the Goertzel window is 1.05
bit periods (`window_size_mul = 1.05`). The matched-filter entry cites
`tools/carrier_frame.py`, which does not exist in the repo or its history.

## PLL characterization before extension

The bit-clock PLL (`src/bitclk.c`) has inertia ramps, a 32-bit transition
history, and DCD hysteresis (lock at 26, unlock at 12 good transitions) —
but nothing measured: lock acquisition time vs. level and noise, behavior
inside the hysteresis band, recovery after squelch bursts, and how much of
the AFSK packet-ear start it misses. First datum from the loss-attribution
counters: DCD never locks at 22050 Hz (0 locked samples even behind 128
preamble flags; it locks at 48000 Hz) and never locks at any C/N on the
static reference channel below the knee of the static reference channel — bits are emitted regardless, so
decoding is unaffected, but the flag is dead exactly where the WA8LMF
tracks live. Intended shape: characterization from
the regression suite (per-case verdicts should record whether DCD ever
locked), and only then decisions about look-ahead/rewind recovery for
frames lost to late lock — the receiver already buffers, so rewinding to
try an earlier start is available and unused. Not the intended shape:
tuning the inertia constants further against one tape; that is the ritual
this file exists to end.

## Per-frame signal-quality estimate: audio SNR, C/N or Eb/N0 reported with each decoded frame

Problem: miniwolf decodes frames but reports nothing about the channel they
arrived through — the receiver already computes per-sample signal power (AGC,
squelch envelopes) and per-bit confidence (`bitclk.signal_quality`), yet a
decoded frame carries no quality figure, so operators and tools cannot tell a
clean decode from one scraped out of the noise. Intended shape: estimate the
received channel quality over each frame's audio span (the demodulators
already see the frame boundaries) and report it with the decode line — audio
SNR, C/N (the repo's noise reference, dB at the IF output), or Eb/N0, one
honest estimator rather than all three. Criticality: a wish — worth doing
because it costs little (the quantities are already computed internally) and
makes every capture, gate line and field report self-describing; the estimator
must be validated against the tools' known-C/N channels (tools/ber.py draws at
labeled C/N) before its numbers are trusted.
